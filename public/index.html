Measuring parliamentary discourse in Hungary

Hungary publishes each sitting of parliament as a PDF. I built an NLP pipeline that combines rules with a language model to extract speakers and their parties, attacks, interruptions and votes.

  • 82–91%accuracy vs. hand-coding
  • ~87,000pages of transcripts
  • 36 years10 parliaments
