How the events were funded

Events were free to attend. Speakers were unpaid, and venues, caterers and video crews donated their services.

  1. SpeakersFounders, investors, scientists and officials spoke without pay.
  2. AudienceYoung founders attended in person or by livestream, with no membership dues or tickets.
  3. PartnersPartners gave venues, catering, streaming and photography in kind, plus sponsorship.
  4. Free eventsA larger audience made it easier to book the next speaker.

Post-lockdown startup gathering at HUB55

The picnic was planned for Margaret Island and moved to HUB55 because of the weather. Each startup and organization had a blanket where visitors could stop and ask about starting a business and raising money. There were also board games, sports and free legal advice.
